Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

VoicePlayer ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you use the VoicePlayer desktop application and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In (Optional): When you sign in via Google OAuth, we request only your basic profile information (openid, email, profile) — specifically your email address and display name.
  • Local Processing: Text-to-speech synthesis with Piper/Kokoro, speech recognition with Whisper, and your queue/archive history are processed locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • To identify your account inside the App and sync preferences.
  • To send product updates, release notes, and useful tips if you opted in during sign-in (you can unsubscribe at any time).

3. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Account email and opt-in preferences are stored securely for product communication.

4. Contact & Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your account data or ask privacy questions, contact us at tikoulf@gmail.com or via Telegram @voiceplayer_ai.
